    Geolocation stays in this browser session. Streets, camera tiles, address searches, and routing use external services. Personal camera reports are saved only in this browser. FlockFree does not store a trip history. When GPS guidance is active, coordinates may be sent to the OSRM demonstration routing service for recalculation.
